# Motion and teaching

Keep an object visible while the narration continues to refer to it. Use a named cue to reveal an object, move information, inspect a value, focus attention or change state. Prefer a visible cause and effect over repeated entrances. Preserve entity IDs and meaningful positions within continuous sequences. Use camera moves sparingly and keep labels readable throughout.

Teach technical meaning through the structure and motion of the relevant data. Let a check visibly inspect indexed cells, a branch comparison highlight the branch being discussed, and a transfer arrive at a visible destination.
