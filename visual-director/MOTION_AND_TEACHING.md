# Motion and teaching reference
These examples describe visual reasoning, not the production JSON schema or fixed topic templates.

## Golden PDF-to-vectors flow
A detailed PDF stack with badge and page content → extracted text splits into chunk cards → chunks feed an embedding model → sample numeric vector rows appear → vectors enter a recognizable layered database cylinder. Preserve object identity and use meaningful dashed routes. The supplied SVG is a static quality target; animate only the relevant portions as the audio progresses. Sample numeric vectors are illustrative, not computed measurements.

## Other mechanisms
- Bloom filter: show input, hash operations, computed indices, indexed bit cells, insertion and membership checks; possible membership is not guaranteed presence. Show backing-store verification for the illustrated false positive.
- Merkle comparison: two recognizable hierarchical trees, meaningful example hashes, matching/mismatching branches, identify differing leaf, transfer data under an appropriate repair policy, recompute hashes. Do not invent which replica is authoritative.
- Baggage: recognizable bag, conveyor route, scanner, tag, controller, diverter and destination. Show physical movement and response to routing; integrate illustration with the mechanism, not as a detached thumbnail.
- Payments: realistic app UI, relevant official logos and transaction states, actual request/response semantics; distinguish illustrative architecture from verified company internals.
- Spatial indexing: map context, cells, meaningful markers, relevant selection or distance operations. Avoid portraying an index as a routing engine.

## Motion language
Progressive reveals, smooth camera focus, path drawing, small luminous packets following the exact route, and meaningful status changes. Author every event from the saved cues. Preserve prior states across continuous sequences. Use restrained highlight glows on active paths and objects and keep idle routes visible at lower contrast. Attach routes to stationary painted object edges, leave from the declared side, and approach the receiver from its declared side. Draw bends in open corridors. Keep labels clear of the route, check every repeat and preserve intentional authored spacing. Use deterministic frame-based motion and continue the visual across sentences where the mechanism is shared.

## QA rubric
Check semantic accuracy, mechanism visibility, fitted text and captions, asset identity, continuity, and exact cue timing. Inspect full-resolution before, action and after frames, including a frame decoded from the final export. Verify the farthest repeats and both connector ports. Check the title, counts and example data against the narration. Review overlap notes at the indicated sequence frames and adjust the authored layout only when needed. Flag ambiguous references and clipping for targeted repair.
